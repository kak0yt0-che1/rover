MISSION DESIGN
A teaching game about space engineering trade-offs.

Design a space mission. Then see if it survives.

Pick a goal, build a small spacecraft, choose a rocket and an orbit. Six limits fight each other: mass, power, budget, data, communications and time.

Play Mission Design 1 to 4 players, about 5 minutes, runs in your browser, nothing to install.

How a round works

  1. Choose a mission and a difficulty.
  2. Build the spacecraft and pick the rocket and orbit. The dashboard shows all six limits live.
  3. Launch. A 30-day mission runs with random events.
  4. Read the report on why it worked or failed. Redesign and fly again.

Bonus: Rover Rules

Program a Mars rover with visual IF / ELSE IF / ELSE blocks and watch it drive over real terrain of Jezero crater (USGS data).
